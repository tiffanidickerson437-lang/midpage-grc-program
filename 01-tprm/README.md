# 01 — Third-party risk

Eighteen disclosed subprocessors, of which five are a different animal.

[`model-provider-tier.md`](model-provider-tier.md) is the argument: Anthropic, Google,
OpenAI, Mistral, and Cohere receive the sensitive payload directly, and every public
confidentiality commitment Midpage makes rides on *their* contracts rather than on any
Midpage-side control. A standard SaaS questionnaire cannot evidence a claim that lives
in somebody else's paper, and an annual reassessment cadence cannot detect a mid-year
change in a provider's retention terms.

The finding that this section anchors is machine-checked in
[`05-stakeholder-management/public-claims-consistency/`](../05-stakeholder-management/public-claims-consistency/):
the two public subprocessor disclosures do not list the same providers, and the longer
list is the one the DPA names.
