# 02 — AI governance

Two files, both about the distance between a promise and a control.

**[`eu-establishment/`](eu-establishment/)** — the finding with the largest gap between
how quiet it looks and how much it costs to get wrong. Midpage's public data-protection
machinery is built for a US company exporting personal data out of the EEA, and Midpage
appears to have an office inside it.

**[`data-use-commitments.md`](data-use-commitments.md)** — three public statements about
what happens to customer data, made on three different surfaces, that a law firm's
outside counsel guidelines will read together and find in tension.

What is deliberately absent: an ISO 42001 readiness roadmap, a NIST AI RMF crosswalk, a
model inventory. Those are the deliverables of an engagement, not observations from
outside, and inventing them here would be inventing facts about systems nobody outside
has seen. The [engine](../generated/engine-bridge.md) renders all three from one config
once there is a config to render from.
