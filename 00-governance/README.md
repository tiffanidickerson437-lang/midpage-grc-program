# 00 — Governance

The research position, made auditable.

Two files, and the order matters.

**[`public-surface-map.md`](public-surface-map.md)** is the sourcing spine. Every claim
anywhere in this repository traces to a row in it, with the URL and the date it was
read. If a finding cannot be traced there, it does not belong here.

**[`open-questions.md`](open-questions.md)** is the other half of the same discipline:
thirteen things that cannot be answered from outside, written down as questions rather
than inferred past. A gap assessment that guesses at these and presents the guesses as
findings is not more thorough — it is less trustworthy, and the first person to check
one will discount all the rest.

## Ground rule

**Gaps are the work, never the criticism.** Everything observed here was observed on a
page Midpage chose to publish, by someone who pays for the product and uses it weekly.
A company of nine people running SOC 2, HIPAA, eighteen subprocessors, four model
providers, and a data-supply business with no compliance headcount has not been
careless. It has been *fast*, which is the correct thing to have been, and the bill for
being fast comes due at the Series A rather than before it.

## Evidence

`evidence_in_repo: none`. This repository contains no Midpage systems evidence, no
account data, and no requested documents. The instruments run against committed, dated
observations of public pages. No output here should be read as though it touched a
Midpage system.
