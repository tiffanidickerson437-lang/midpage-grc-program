# 05 — Stakeholder management

Claims hygiene as a revenue control.

**[`public-claims-consistency/`](public-claims-consistency/)** — three instruments,
nine findings, 93 tests. Each tool compares what Midpage says on one public surface
against what Midpage says on another and reports the disagreement. None of them asserts
what is true.

**[`trust-collateral.md`](trust-collateral.md)** — two cheaper findings that cost
nothing to fix and are noticed by exactly the wrong people: a compliance category error
on a sales page, and the absence of any end-user documentation or changelog on a product
whose users are lawyers.

The reason all of this lives in a compliance repository rather than a marketing one: a
reviewer who catches a marketing page contradicting another marketing page starts
discounting every unattested number on the site, including the ones in the security
section. Those are the numbers that cost money to be wrong about.
