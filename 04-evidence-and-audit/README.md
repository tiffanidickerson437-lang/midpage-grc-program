# 04 — Evidence and audit

One file: [`claims-to-evidence.md`](claims-to-evidence.md).

It takes the eleven specific commitments Midpage makes on public pages and asks, for
each, what would evidence it. Five of them fall inside what a SOC 2 Type II is built to
cover. Six do not — and the six are the AI-specific ones, which is to say the ones a law
firm's general counsel actually asks about.

That inversion is the finding: **evidence for the claims nobody doubts, assertions for
the claims everybody asks about.** It is also entirely normal, because no framework
Midpage currently holds has an AI criterion, so no auditor has asked yet.

`evidence_in_repo: none`. Nothing here is Midpage evidence. This is a map of what
evidence would look like, drawn from claims Midpage published itself.
