# 04 — Evidence and audit

Two files.

[`claims-to-evidence.md`](claims-to-evidence.md) takes the eleven commitments Midpage
makes on public pages and asks, for each, what would evidence it.

[`audit-clock.md`](audit-clock.md) asks when the SOC 2 Type II window actually closes.
It is not publicly determinable, which is the finding — and along the way it establishes
that the security page's own "Last updated" stamp is stale by at least two months,
across a revision that added a compliance claim.

Of those eleven commitments, five fall inside what a SOC 2 Type II is built to cover.
Six do not — and the six are the AI-specific ones, which is to say the ones a law firm's
general counsel actually asks about.

That inversion is the finding: **evidence for the claims nobody doubts, assertions for
the claims everybody asks about.** It is also entirely normal, because no framework
Midpage currently holds has an AI criterion, so no auditor has asked yet.

`evidence_in_repo: none`. Nothing here is Midpage evidence. This is a map of what
evidence would look like, drawn from claims Midpage published itself.
